Ke Zhou

Hi! I'm a first-year Computer Science Mphil Student at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology(GZ).

My research interests lie in intersection of extended reality(XR) and human-centered intelligence with a particular focus on leveraging efficient, data-driven techniques to enhance multimodal experiences on portable XR devices. I aim to employ AI-driven approaches to perceive and interpret user-centric data(e.g., eye tracking, hand gestures, physiological signals) and environmental information (e.g., physical properties, semantics) to 1) Designing personalized AI-assisted techniques that offer guidance and information display to facilitate user experience in XR. 2) Developing efficient rendering techniques that combining novel rendering algorithms and edge computing to drastically reduce computational overhead and latency while maintaining hyper-realistic visual fidelity. Ultimately, my goal is to build next-generation extended reality systems that are both intelligent and immersive, integrating the virtual and physical world seamlessly.

Prior to HKUST(GZ), I received my Bachelor's degree at Chongqing University, During my undergraduate years, I was honored to be advised by Prof. Wai Tong from Texas A&M University and Prof. Teng Han from ISCAS

I am actively seeking PhD opportunities for Fall 2026 admission!
